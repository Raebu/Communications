1. Data we process

Depending on how the service is used, we process account and authentication data, business profile and verification data, contact details, subscription and billing references, assigned-number and call-routing data, SMS conversation content and delivery metadata, support communications, audit/security records, service usage, and configuration for optional AI or integration features.

2. Why we use it

We process data to provide and secure the service, verify businesses and authorised users, administer subscriptions, route communications, prevent fraud and abuse, meet legal and regulatory obligations, provide support, and improve reliability. Our lawful bases may include performance of a contract, compliance with legal obligations, legitimate interests in operating and securing the service, and consent where consent is specifically required.

3. Verification data

Director identity checks may be performed by Stripe Identity. Identity documents and selfie images used by that provider are not uploaded to Raeburn Connect. We may retain verification status, provider references and evidence needed to demonstrate the result of a check.

4. Service providers

We use service providers for telecommunications, payment processing, hosting/database infrastructure, transactional email, identity verification and security. These providers process information under their own terms and applicable data-protection obligations. International transfers, where they occur, are handled using applicable safeguards.

5. Communications data

Business users control the content they send through the service and are responsible for having an appropriate basis to contact recipients. We process communications content and metadata so the requested communications service can operate and so delivery, opt-out, security and support functions work correctly.

6. Retention

Default application settings retain message content for 90 days and voice-related application data for 30 days unless a different lawful retention period is configured or required. Account, billing, verification, security and audit records may need to be kept longer for legal, regulatory, fraud-prevention, accounting or dispute purposes.

7. Security

We use technical and organisational measures including encrypted secrets, access controls, signed provider webhooks, transport encryption, tenant separation, security logging and restricted administrative functions. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

8. Your rights

UK data-protection law may give you rights to access, correct, erase or restrict personal data, object to certain processing, and receive portable data in applicable cases. Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw that consent without affecting earlier lawful processing. To exercise a right, contact the support address above.

9. Complaints

You may raise a privacy concern with us first. You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office if you believe your data-protection rights have been infringed.

10. Changes

We may update this policy to reflect changes to the service, law or providers. The current version and effective date will be shown on this page.
